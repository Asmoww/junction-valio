What your agent does, step by step

1Look upSearch Valio's catalog, then live price and stock for a shortlist.search_products
2Get a signed offerA fixed price for 15 minutes, signed by Valio. Not a negotiation.request_offer
3Place the orderVahti runs checks 4–6. Placed, sent to a person, or stopped.place_order
4Follow upRead the decision and the audit trail. Only a person approves.get_order

The same steps over MCP, REST (OpenAPI) and A2A. An inquiry-only token can look up and price but never order. What Vahti checks on every order
